# SG4 - Understanding Classes and Objects
## Family Member
## The class “Family Member” is a member of a family in a household. 
## Properties
| Property | Data Type | Description | Visibility | Why Public/Private? | 

- | Age | Integer | The family member's age. | Private | Because age can be used in bad ways |

+ | Role | String  | Role of the family member in the household. | Public | Helpful to know |

+ | Name  | String | The name of the family member. | Public | Helpful to know | 

- | Prescence | Boolean | If the family member is present in the household or not. | Private | Can be helpful but house might be robbed when nobody is present |

## Methods
| Method | Description |

| talk() | The family member's method of vocally giving information.|

| eat() | The family member's method of consuming food. |

| drink() | The family member's method of consuming liquids. |

## Design Explanation
### I chose this class because it is one of the most important things in life.
### The most important property is role, since it makes up the family member's role in life as a sister, brother, mother, and so on.
### The most useful method in my opinion is talk(), since it is the primary way of delivering informative messages.
